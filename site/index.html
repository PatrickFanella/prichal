Prichal Private alpha

Причал · Account hosting

Your server.
We keep it running.

Prichal hosts your account's home server on the open network Bluesky runs on. Your name, your posts and your followers live on a server that is yours. We handle the machine.

Prichal is not open for sign-up yet. We are testing it privately.

Who it is for

Musicians and creators

Use your own name as your username, such as @yourband.com. Keep posting from Bluesky like always. Your audience follows you, not an account a platform company holds.

Organizations and collectives

Give your community a home on the network under your name. Member accounts are in development and not available yet.

Anyone who wants their own server

Same app, same followers, on a server run for you instead of by you.

In plain terms

What you get
A personal data server (PDS) for your account, run and maintained for you.
What stays public
Posts on the open network are public, wherever your server runs.
Your data
Your account and posts can move to another host. We will document how before we open.
